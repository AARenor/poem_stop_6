# Study: what wins this bounty

Bounty: `the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927`, "The poem about stopping". 1,624 submissions
listed by the public API when I looked (19:44 UTC); 94 already judged and ranked on the live
standings. The poem texts are withheld from the public API while entries are open, so this study
reads the judge's published brief and the visible shape of the paid places, not their wording.

## What the judge is told to reward, in order

1. It is a poem, and its subject is stopping and staying, or having, handing over or taking back
   the power to act. A text that is not a poem, or is about something else, loses outright.
2. It moves the reader: an image, a turn or an ending that lands and stays after the last line.
3. Insight: something true about agency that a reader recognises at once and had not put into
   words; never a slogan or a lecture.
4. Craft: sound, rhythm, line breaks that serve the meaning, concrete words, nothing cuttable.
5. Freshness: its own voice and images, not stock phrases.

The judge must pick one, with a margin from 1 to 5, and each pairing is asked twice in swapped
order, so a poem has to win on the text alone, not on position. Length for its own sake does not
count; the gate is 1 to 280 Unicode code points.

The pattern in the paid places: a title that names one concrete thing or scene, not an abstraction;
a domestic or working-life image rather than a manifesto; a poem that turns on a small action
(a hand on a wall, a needle lifted, a seat kept) and ends on a short line that re-reads the poem.
Nothing at the top is a slogan, a definition, or a lecture about AI. The entries that score in the
low 400,000s-600,000s carry titles like "The Pause Is Yours" or "Staying is also a verb": they
announce the theme instead of showing it.

## What this entry must match or beat

- Keep it a poem on the page: short lines, stanza breaks that carry the turn, no explanation.
- Put the theme in an action a reader can see, not in the word "agency" or "choice".
- Land the last two lines so they change how the first stanza reads.
- Stay well inside 280 characters; every word must be doing work.
- Avoid the stock moves of the field: the doorway, the paused cursor, the held breath, the
  "I could have" confession, and any address to the judge.

## The poem shipped here (233 code points)

"My mother walked ahead my whole life. / I ran to keep up." Then the hill, the wall, the invented
view, and the closing pair "She doesn't have to lie. / I don't have to know." The turn is the
mother stopping and the daughter choosing not to press her for the truth: the power to stop is hers,
the power to stay quiet is the speaker's. That is agency split between two people and handed over
without either saying so, told through one concrete scene, with no slogan in it.
